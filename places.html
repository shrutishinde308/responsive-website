Top Places to Visit in Pune

Shaniwar Wada

Famous historical fort and a major attraction of Pune.

Aga Khan Palace

A beautiful historical monument connected with Indian history.

Sinhagad Fort

A popular hill fort offering beautiful views and trekking.

Parvati Hill

A peaceful place with a temple and beautiful city views.

Pataleshwar Cave Temple

An ancient rock-cut temple located in the heart of Pune.

Osho Garden

A peaceful green garden suitable for relaxation.

Lal Mahal

An important historical place associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Empress Garden

A beautiful garden with greenery and peaceful surroundings.

Khadakwasla Dam

A popular spot to enjoy nature and beautiful scenery.

Rajgad Fort

A famous fort and a popular destination for trekking.
